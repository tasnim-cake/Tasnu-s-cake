About Cake 🍰

Cake is one of the most loved desserts around the world. It can be made in many different flavors, shapes and sizes. Chocolate, vanilla, strawberry, red velvet and many other flavors make cake perfect for different occasions.

🏡 What Is Homemade Cake?

Homemade cake is a cake prepared at home using ingredients such as flour, eggs, sugar, butter or oil, milk and baking powder. Making cake at home allows us to choose our favorite flavors and decorations.

💕 Why We Love Homemade Cake

Homemade cakes are special because they are made with care and creativity. Baking at home can be a fun activity with family and friends, and sharing a freshly baked cake can make celebrations even more memorable.

🎉 Perfect For Every Occasion

Homemade cakes are wonderful for birthdays, weddings, festivals, family gatherings and other celebrations. You can also bake a simple cake just to enjoy with a cup of tea or coffee.

🍓 Different Cake Flavors

There are many delicious cake flavors to try, including chocolate cake, vanilla cake, red velvet cake, lemon cake, mango cake, strawberry cake, coconut cake and coffee cake.

❤️ The Sweetest Part

The best thing about homemade cake is not only its taste, but also the happiness of making and sharing something special with the people we love.

🍰 10 Delicious Cake Recipes

Easy homemade cakes for everyone

Home Recipes

1. 🎂 Vanilla Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Mix the ingredients until smooth. Pour into a cake pan and bake at 180°C for about 30–35 minutes.

2. 🍫 Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Combine all ingredients and mix well. Pour into a greased pan and bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

3. ❤️ Red Velvet Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • Red food coloring
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Mix everything into a smooth batter. Add red coloring, pour into a pan and bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

4. 🍋 Lemon Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • Lemon zest

Method

Mix the ingredients well. Pour into a cake pan and bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

5. 🥭 Mango Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup mango puree
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Mix the mango puree with the other ingredients. Pour into a pan and bake at 180°C for about 30–35 minutes.

6. 🍪 Cookies & Cream Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • Crushed chocolate cookies
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Prepare the cake batter and fold in crushed cookies. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

7. ☕ Coffee Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon instant coffee
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Dissolve coffee in a little warm milk. Mix with the remaining ingredients and bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

8. 🥥 Coconut Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup shredded coconut
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Mix the ingredients together and pour into a cake pan. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

9. 🍓 Strawberry Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup chopped strawberries
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Prepare the batter and gently mix in strawberries. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

10. 🍯 Honey Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

Mix the ingredients until smooth. Pour into a greased pan and bake at 180°C for about 30–35 minutes.
