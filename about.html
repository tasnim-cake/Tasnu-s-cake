About Cakes

Cakes have changed over time, but they have remained a favorite food for celebrations around the world.

📜

A Short History

Early cakes were often simple sweetened breads. Over centuries, baking methods and ingredients improved, leading to the soft and decorated cakes we enjoy today.

🌎

Cakes Around the World

Different cultures have their own special cakes, from light sponge cakes to rich chocolate desserts and fruit-filled treats.

🎂

Popular Types

Some popular varieties include sponge cake, chocolate cake, cheesecake, carrot cake, red velvet cake and fruit cake.

Fun Cake Facts

  • Cakes can be decorated with fruit, cream, chocolate, nuts and many other toppings.
  • Birthday cakes are a popular tradition in many countries.
  • The word “cake” is connected to old words for baked foods.
  • Baking is a great way to learn about measurement, timing and how ingredients work together.
