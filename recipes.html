Simple Cake Recipes

Here are two beginner-friendly recipes you can try at home with adult supervision when using an oven.

🍫 Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  3. Add eggs, milk and oil, then mix until smooth.
  4. Pour into a greased cake pan.
  5. Bake for about 30–35 minutes.
  6. Let it cool before decorating.
Tip: Add chocolate chips or fresh berries for extra flavor.

🍦 Vanilla Cake

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups flour
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar together.
  3. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix in flour and baking powder, alternating with milk.
  5. Pour into a greased pan.
  6. Bake for about 25–30 minutes and cool.
Tip: Top with whipped cream and fruit for a fresh finish.
