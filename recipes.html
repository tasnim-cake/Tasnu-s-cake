🍰 10 Homemade Cake Recipes

Simple, delicious and easy cakes you can make at home!

1. 🎂 Classic Vanilla Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour and baking powder.
  3. Beat butter and sugar until smooth.
  4. Add eggs, milk and vanilla.
  5. Combine the wet and dry ingredients.
  6. Pour into a greased cake pan.
  7. Bake for about 30–35 minutes.
💡 Tip: Let the cake cool before decorating.

2. 🍫 Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, cocoa powder and baking powder.
  2. Beat eggs, sugar, milk and oil.
  3. Combine everything into a smooth batter.
  4. Pour into a cake pan.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

3. ❤️ Red Velvet Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • Red food coloring
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda

Instructions

  1. Mix the dry ingredients.
  2. Beat butter and sugar together.
  3. Add eggs and buttermilk.
  4. Add cocoa and red food coloring.
  5. Mix everything together.
  6. Bake at 180°C for about 30 minutes.

4. 🍋 Lemon Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • Lemon zest
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Combine flour and baking powder.
  2. Beat butter and sugar.
  3. Add eggs, milk, lemon juice and zest.
  4. Mix until smooth.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

5. 🥭 Mango Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup mango puree

  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix flour and baking powder.
  2. Mix mango puree, sugar, eggs and oil.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
  4. Pour into a cake pan.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

6. 🍪 Cookies & Cream Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 8–10 crushed chocolate cookies
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Prepare a basic cake batter.
  2. Fold in the crushed cookies.
  3. Pour into a greased pan.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.
  5. Cool and decorate with cookie crumbs.

7. 🥥 Coconut Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup butter

  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

9. 🍒 Cherry Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup chopped cherries
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  4. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.

10. 🫐 Blueberry Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix flour and baking powder.
  2. Beat butter and sugar.
  3. Add eggs and milk.
  4. Fold in the blueberries gently.
  5. Pour into a cake pan.
  6. Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes.
💡 Tip: Serve with fresh blueberries for extra flavor.
