🧁 Red Velvet Delight

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon red food coloring
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar together.
  3. Add the eggs, vanilla and red food coloring.
  4. Mix the flour, cocoa powder and baking soda separately.
  5. Add the dry ingredients and buttermilk gradually.
  6. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  7. Bake for about 30–35 minutes.
  8. Let the cake cool before decorating.
💡 Tip: Cream cheese frosting makes a delicious topping.

🍋 Lemon Sunshine Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Beat butter and sugar until creamy.
  3. Add the eggs, vanilla and lemon zest.
  4. Add flour and baking powder.
  5. Mix in the milk and lemon juice.
  6. Pour into a greased cake pan.
  7. Bake for about 30–35 minutes.
  8. Cool before serving.
💡 Tip: Add a little lemon glaze on top for extra flavor.

🥭 Mango Magic Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup mango puree
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Mix the eggs, sugar and oil.
  3. Add mango puree and vanilla.
  4. Add flour and baking powder gradually.
  5. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  6. Pour into a greased cake pan.
  7. Bake for about 30–35 minutes.
  8. Cool before decorating.
💡 Tip: Decorate with fresh mango pieces and whipped cream.

🍪 Cookies & Cream Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 10 chocolate sandwich cookies, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix in flour and baking powder.
  5. Add the milk gradually.
  6. Fold in the crushed cookies.
  7. Pour into a greased cake pan.
  8. Bake for 30–35 minutes.
💡 Tip: Add whipped cream and cookie crumbs on top.

☕ Coffee Mocha Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon instant coffee
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Cream butter and sugar together.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix flour, cocoa and baking powder.
  5. Dissolve coffee in the milk.
  6. Add the milk mixture to the batter.
  7. Pour into a greased cake pan.
  8. Bake for 30–35 minutes.
💡 Tip: Chocolate frosting pairs wonderfully with this cake.
